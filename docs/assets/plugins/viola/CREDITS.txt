This pack includes one separately recorded bowed mezzo-forte note for
each semitone from C3 through C7. TinySOL corrected the tuning of some
takes. For Alto, each take was excerpted at the bow attack, trimmed to
one second, level-adjusted, faded, and converted to 32 kHz mono 16-bit
PCM WAV. The filenames below identify the original takes in TinySOL's
Strings/Viola/ordinario directory.

Picture: Viola by August Martin Gemünder, American, 1893. The Metropolitan Museum of
Art, New York, Gift of A. Gemunder & Sons, 1945 (45.71.3). Photograph released
by the museum under its Open Access program (Creative Commons Zero).
https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/504403
The picture of the back, images/viola-back.webp, is the museum's second photograph of
the same viola (DP-21004-002). For Alto, each photograph was cut out from its studio
backdrop, turned so the neck points left, and scaled to 2040 by 768 pixels. The museum lists the viola as
67 cm long overall with a 36.9 cm vibrating string length.

The tile icon, icons/viola.svg, is the outline of the front photograph, with its f-holes, fingerboard, and tailpiece cut out, traced for Alto.
